8.10 C2 Certification
DJI Matrice 4T / DJI Matrice 4E is comply with C2 certification, there are some
requirements and restrictions when using DJI Matrice 4T / DJI Matrice 4E in European
Economic Area (EEA, i.e. EU plus Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein). DJI Matrice 4T / DJI
Matrice 4E and its similar products are distinguished by model name.

MTOM Statement
The MTOM of DJI Matrice 4T / DJI Matrice 4E (Model M4T/M4E) is 1430 g to comply with C2
requirement.
You must follow the instructions below to comply with the MTOM requirements for each
model:
•
Make sure that when installing any external devices, the total weight of the aircraft
does not exceed the maximum takeoff weight (1430 g). In addition, the external
devices must be installed in a location that ensures the center of gravity is maintained
within the range of the aircraft's top shell to keep the aircraft stable, and that the
vision systems, infrared sensing systems, and auxiliary lights are not blocked. Make
sure that the MTOM is not larger than 1430 g for any flight.
