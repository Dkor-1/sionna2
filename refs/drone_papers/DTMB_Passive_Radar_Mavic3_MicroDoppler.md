# DTMB-based passive radar — DJI Mavic 3 rotor micro-Doppler (passive bistatic)
- **Paradigm**: passive bistatic radar, DTMB illuminator of opportunity
- **Target**: **DJI Mavic 3** (closest heavily-used Mavic 4 Pro predecessor)
- **Key**: experimentally extracted **rotor micro-Doppler** of Mavic 3 (cooperative target).
  Complemented by DVB-T2 / VNA micro-Doppler studies of Phantom-series.
- **우리 대조**: 패시브+마이크로도플러+Mavic 계열 = 우리 패러다임과 가장 근접한 실측 레퍼런스.
