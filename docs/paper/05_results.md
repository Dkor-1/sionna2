### 방법 문단 (논문 이관용)

Detection range is solved in free space for a bistatic pair with baseline L = 500 m, target altitude 60 m, scene azimuth 90 deg, declared EIRP 63 dBm, receive gain 10 dBi, noise figure 5 dB, CPI 100 ms and N = 1 receive element. The three illuminators are the always-on reference signal of each standard (WiFi VHT-LTF at 5.21 GHz, LTE CRS at 1.843 GHz, 5G NR SSB at 3.5 GHz), each correlated over its own reference bandwidth, at equal power spectral density. The threshold is set by driving the empirical false-alarm rate to 1e-04 in the same free-space geometry, and the threshold measured for WiFi is shared by all three solves. Cross sections come from a per-part material physical-optics kernel with ray-traced occlusion evaluated on an aspect grid; the frequency slope of the azimuth-mean level is anchored to the measured 0.210 dB/GHz of Das et al. while the aspect pattern and the three-band mean level are computed. Bistatic results hold for beta <= 45 deg. Doppler blindness is evaluated over 720 headings at 5 m/s with a zero-Doppler guard of 1.5 bins (applied) and 2.5 bins (declared).

버전 — `Python 3.12.13` · `NumPy 2.5.0` · `Matplotlib 3.11.0` · `PyTorch 2.12.1` · `Sionna 2.0.1`
