## 1. What the adapter does

| Step | Rule |
|---|---|
| baseband gain | `amp = a * exp(-2j*pi*fc*tau)` from the sidecar's passband `a`; applied once, here |
| delay | `tau_s = tau`, the absolute round-trip delay; never re-centred or normalised |
| Doppler | `f_hz = 0` for every RT path: the sweep stores none (`unpack(want_doppler=False)`) |
| pose | one stored pose per receiver frame; no path is matched or interpolated across poses |
| classes | `drone_only` / `env_only` / `mixed` from the sidecar `part` names; used only after the receiver returns |

Carrier for the baseband conversion: 3.5 GHz, taken from the stored cell (no `_fc` tag in the arm name, so the sweep's convention carrier applies (benchmark/elevation_sweep_md.py, FC = 3.5e9)). The receiver bands below use the same value and the run stops if they differ. 3.5 GHz is the working assumption of these cells, not a decided band.

Because every RT path here is at zero Doppler, slow-time mean removal would delete the whole channel; section 3 therefore runs the receiver with `static_removal = False` and noise variance 0.0. Intra-frame motion, the pose-rate/symbol-rate mismatch and any time interpolation are out of this file's scope.

## 5. Finite bursts: the circular-delay channel against a linear stream

Band `bw20` (occupied 18.39 MHz), FFT 1024, CP 72 samples = 351.3 m of round-trip range, 256 transmitted symbols on a 512-slot grid in both schedules. Paired across the schedules and across the two channel models: the payload, the two path phases, the Doppler, the number of transmitted symbols and the noise draw. Not paired, by construction: the elapsed span. The continuous schedule sends its 256 symbols in 256 consecutive slots (9.133 ms) and the two-burst schedule splits them into two blocks at the two ends of the grid, spanning 512 slots (18.27 ms). That difference is the burst condition being tested, and the Doppler bin below is read against the transmitted symbol count, not against that span.

The Doppler-zero columns repeat each row with `f_hz = 0` on both paths and nothing else changed. `field_residual_db` compares a per-symbol channel estimate with an analytic response that holds one Doppler phase per symbol, so a moving target leaves an intra-symbol-Doppler floor in both models; the Doppler-zero column removes that floor and leaves the delay and CP behaviour alone.

## 6. What is explained, and what failed

- **Complex phase and amplitude - measured, passed.** Over 8192 dumped positions in the four cells the largest `path_sum_rel_err` is 0, against this file's threshold 0.0001; in the weakest cell 100 % of the positions reproduce the stored `E` bit for bit, because the adapter repeats the sweep's own arithmetic on the sweep's own stored numbers. An order-independent sum leaves 4.06e-16, and `sum |a| / |E|` reaches 5.92, so summation order is not carrying the result. What this establishes: the carrier convention (one `exp(-2j*pi*fc*tau)`, not two), the path selection, and that the sidecar list is complete for these cells. What it does not establish: that the traced field is right - that is a measurement question.
- **Time axis - measured, passed.** CFR channel against time-domain channel: 40 of 40 rows are within the stated tolerance; the worst row differs by 0.000133 in the map, 0.000488 m in peak range and 0.000577 dB in peak power.
- **Delay axis - measured.** Collapsing the list into the single stored `E` at the nominal delay, instead of keeping each path at its own delay, raises the drone-range cell (relative to that map's own maximum) by 10.2 to 14.5 dB over the 4 ground rows and 0 to 0.155 dB over the 4 open-sky rows (band and position as tabulated in section 4). Collapsing puts every path at the nominal drone delay, so the cell holds the whole field by construction; that is a property of the collapsed model, not a gain.
- **Residual floor - explained.** With the target moving, both models sit at -60.7 dB for delays inside the CP. The Doppler-zero control drops the same rows to -302 dB, so that floor is the intra-symbol Doppler of a per-symbol channel estimate, not a difference between the two channel models.
- **Beyond the cyclic prefix - a stress result, not a code defect.** At 115 samples (1.6 of the CP, 561.1 m) the field residual rises to -30.9 dB. That target also sits past the receiver's delay grid, which stops at 351.3 m (the CP), so no cell of the map belongs to it: the `delay_bias_m` of those rows is where a spurious peak fell (503.6 m here, moving by 308 m across 8 payload draws), not a measured bias, and it is not evidence of how far off a detection would be. Three things say stress rather than bug: the two models, which differ only in whether the stream wraps, break together (the largest linear-circular gap in `field_residual_db` over all rows is 0.0194 dB); the Doppler-zero control stays at -30.9 dB where the inside-CP rows fall to -302 dB, so the residual follows the delay, not the Doppler; and `usable_fft_fraction` is 0.958, i.e. part of every FFT window is no longer that symbol's own cyclic extension.
