# Purpose. runners/jobs_0962 asks whether the drone-ground ghost follows a computed two-ray LEVEL over grazing angle. It cannot ask about
#   PHASE: every one of its cells fixes the drone-radar-ground geometry, so the propagation phase of the ground-reflected legs is one
#   number per cell, and the rotor motion only turns the phase of individual drone paths inside that fixed geometry. The receiver and the
#   tracker need the other half: the ghost adds to the direct echo coherently, so the in-bin field fades as that relative phase turns, and
#   0962's fade columns are a computed span (+5.0 / −7.0 dB at cell A, point target), never an observed one.
#   This file turns that phase on purpose, by the only knob the runner has, and reads the class phases against the computed rate.
#   What the result changes: if the class phases turn at the computed rate and the levels stay inside 0962's tolerance, the fade of the
#   in-bin field is COMPUTED for any height and range from the listed class amplitudes plus two-ray geometry, and no further ground-only
#   height, range or elevation cell is bought — the tracker's fade model is a formula with cell-measured coefficients. If they do not,
#   the fade model has to come from cells, and the first thing that is written is where the computed model breaks, not a new sweep.
#

#   drone at the origin; benchmark/report15_probe.py place() puts the radar at center + range·(cos el·cos az, cos el·sin az, sin el),
#   ⚠and the sweep calls place(..., baseline=0.0) at elevation_sweep_md.py:1071, so TX and RX are CO-LOCATED and report15_probe's module
#   default BASELINE_M = 0.20 m is NOT used by this sweep. So --env-alt moves the GROUND, and therefore moves the drone AND
#   the radar relative to the ground by the same amount, while their 30 m separation, their −5° elevation and the drone's aspect towards
