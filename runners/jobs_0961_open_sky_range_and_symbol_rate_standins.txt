# 0961 — can the receiver's open-sky inputs come from 15 m, 19.7 kHz cells? (A) With the same number of rays on the drone, does range
#        (15-100 m) change the drone's level, rotor-to-body ratio or rotor lines beyond seed-based thresholds? (B) Can a 19.7 kHz record be
#        taken to the OFDM pilot's symbol times (28,029 Hz) by interpolation? Open sky, looking up 15°, tr38901 aimed, canonical slab.
#        (2026-09-17 ~22:00 KST, queue judge; (A) merged from the decision-value design, (B) replaces a 39.4 kHz / 5.8 GHz design;
#        corrected by the adversarial check ~22:50 KST: 100 m seed pair added as lines 17-18, re-solve floors added to (B))
#
# Launch preconditions (main session).
#   (1) runners/jobs_0960 G0 passed (thickness flags change the cell).
#   (2) Readout script on CPU before any shard is read.
#   (3) Start before runners/jobs_0962: reading R2 here is one of the two ways 0962's 30 m cells get their ray-density check.
#
# Why.
#   (A) The main session's queue verdict of 2026-09-17 (conversation record, not a repo file) puts the range claim at 100 m or less and says
#       absolute SNR comes from the link-budget ledger, while ray tracing supplies relative quantities. Rays leave the radar over the whole
#       sphere, so the paths on the drone scale as rays/R² (counted from stored 2.1.0 shards, open sky 15 m el −60 d1: median npaths 50 / 208 /
#       2,001 at 1e8 / 4e8 / 4e9 rays, 5.0-5.2e-7 per ray; open sky 15 m el −15 d2 tr38901 at 4e9: 1,316 per position, so about 30 at 100 m),
#       and 4e9 is next to the 32-bit ceiling (4,294,967,295). If range changes a cell only through ray density, the receiver takes 15 m
#       cells for 30-100 m with the level scaled by R⁴ (computed, labelled), and no field-range cell is bought. runners/jobs_0957 reading C
#       (30 m at 2e9 vs 4e9, 0.1 m slab) is a one-step version of this; nothing on 2.1.0 has landed at any range other than 15 m.
#   (B) Every stored record and every path list has positions 1/19,700 s apart; the pilot numerology (docs/OFDM_PILOT_0917.md: 30 kHz, FFT 1024,
#       CP 72) has symbols 1/28,029 s apart, so the receiver has to interpolate between positions (strategy §6.2 step 6). Scratch recount on
#       stored 2.0.1 dense records (iso, 0.1 m slab, open sky, 15 m; another design's scratch, not a ledger, do not cite): the linear-
#       interpolation residual at skipped positions falls only 2-2.5 dB per halving of the spacing, about −18 dB relative to the rotor part at
#       19.7 kHz spacing. Rechecked by the adversarial check on the stored 2.0.1 dense records (iso, 0.1 m slab, open sky, 15 m, d2; scratch,
#       not a ledger): prf 39,400 el −15: −17.7 / −15.4 / −12.0 dB at 19,700 / 9,850 / 4,925 Hz spacing; prf 157,600 el −30: −28.2 / −26.1 /
#       −23.9 dB at 78,800 / 39,400 / 19,700 Hz; share of AC power above 9,850 Hz 0.0055 (el −15) and 0.0022 (el −30). A residual that falls
#       only 2-2.5 dB per halving is what a per-position floor (paths listed at one position and not the next) would give, not band-limited
#       interpolation error — a reading, not measured; hence the re-solve floors in T1. The thin propeller lowers the rotor part (0960), so
#       the relative residual may be larger here. A symbol-rate record answers it directly at the canonical slab; its path lists are kept
#       because the receiver reads per-path a and τ, and if T1 crosses they are the symbol-rate channel.
#

# Read-out — fixed before any shard is read. Pre-set working thresholds unless a seed difference is named; inside them «threshold not crossed»,
#   never «no effect», «converged» or «equivalent». contrast_raw is computed on n4096 records only and never placed next to n8192 values.
#   R0 premise: hits_per_pose / (spp / R²) within ±10 % of its median over the cells of (A) → otherwise no range reading is written; cells listed.
#   R1 range at matched rays, per group: the largest pairwise |Δ| of lvl_at_15m_db, ac_to_static_db and contrast_raw against

#   R3 beside runners/jobs_0957 reading C (if landed): its |Δ| next to the 1e9 → 4e9 step here and the 15 m seed difference.
#   T1 (B): first the floor. interp_resid_db ≤ rerun_floor_db + 3 dB → «the interpolation residual is not told apart from re-solving the same
#      positions»: the receiver interpolates, and the per-position floor is carried as a labelled model floor (a symbol-rate record is solved
#      the same way and carries it too). If rerun_floor_db is missing (0957 not landed), seed_floor_db is used and the sentence says «against a
#      seed-change floor, an upper bound». Otherwise: interp_resid_db (linear) ≤ −20 dB → «19.7 kHz records interpolated to the pilot symbol
#      times stay 20 dB below the rotor part (threshold not crossed)»: the receiver interpolates. > −10 dB → «rotor-part readouts at the pilot
#      symbol rate need symbol-rate records (threshold crossed)». In between → both numbers written; the receiver uses symbol-rate records for
#      rotor-part readouts and interpolation for levels.
